## 1. What geomancy actually is

Geomancy (Gk. *gē + manteía*, "earth-divination"; Lat. *geomantia*) is itself a translation. The art is
Arab–Islamic: **عِلْم الرَّمْل — *ʿilm al-raml*, "the science of the sand"** (also *khaṭṭ al-raml*, the
lining of the sand; *ḍarb al-raml*, the striking of the sand). Foundational author: the 13th-c. Berber master
**muḥammad al-Zanātī (محمد الزناتي)**, *Kitāb al-faṣl fī uṣūl ʿilm al-raml*. The system itself is older — a
strongly astrological synthesis assembled in southern Mesopotamia in an Ismāʿīlī milieu around the 10th c.,
absorbing Hellenic, Persian, and Indian material. Generation: the four **الأُمَّهات** *al-ummahāt* (Mothers,
struck into the sand) → **البَنات** *al-banāt* (Daughters, by transposition) → nieces/witnesses/judge by
addition. *(Savage-Smith & Smith, *Islamic Geomancy and a 13th-Century Divinatory Device*; Princeton/ezb
"Geomancy Step-by-Step".)*

## 2. The *dākhil / khārij* grammar — the Arabic names encode the symmetry the Latin scattered

The most important structural fact, and a genuinely beautiful one: **three of the figure-pairs the European
tradition names with unrelated metaphors are, in Arabic, transparent "inner / outer" (دَاخِل / خَارِج,
*dākhil/khārij*) pairs** — and those pairs are exactly the **reflection (upside-down) pairs** the locked
table verifies structurally.

| Reflection pair (verified) | Arabic | literal | Latin (scattered) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Caput ↔ Cauda Draconis | *ʿAtaba al-dākhila / al-khārija* (العتبة الداخلة/الخارجة) | **inner / outer threshold** | "Dragon's Head / Tail" (a lunar-node astrology overlay) |
| Fortuna Major ↔ Minor | *Nuṣra al-dākhil / al-khārij* (نصرة الداخل/الخارج) | **inner / outer victory** | "greater / lesser fortune" |
| Acquisitio ↔ Amissio | *Qabḍ al-dākhil / al-khārij* (قبض الداخل/الخارج) | **inner / outer grasping** | "gain / loss" |

The Arabic naming is *more* faithful to the 2⁴ form the tool is built on: the inner/outer morphology mirrors
the parity structure of the dot-patterns. The Latin kept the geometry and lost the grammar. *(Savage-Smith
device name-set; Digital Ambler, "Same Figures, Different Names"; Farrell, "Changing the dragons".)*

## 4. Three readings the Latin transmission flattened (modern reconstruction — Farrell; interpretive, not medieval-verbatim)

- **Via / *Ṭāriq*** — the Latin "road" kept a dead highway-metaphor and lost the living one: *the night-comer,
  the solitary star in the dark* — illumination, the lone seeker, against *al-Jamāʿa* the crowd.
- **Carcer / *ʿUqla*** — not only "prison": a binding-knot, discipline, steadfastness, even a binding pact.
- **Albus / *Bayāḍ*** — the Latin reads white as wise *old age* (an ending); the Arabic leans to the **blank
  page, the not-yet-written, a beginning**. A polarity the Latin reversed.

## 5. The formalism — the casting math is the group (ℤ/2)⁴ (Jaulin 1966)

**Robert Jaulin, *La Géomancie: analyse formelle*** (Mouton/EPHE, 1966; math notes by Dejean & Ferry) gave
geomancy its rigorous structural account. It does **not correct** this tool's 2⁴ build — it *vindicates* it
and supplies the algebraic name:

- Each figure = a **4-tuple over {single, double}** = a vector in **(ℤ/2)⁴**. The 16 are the complete
  enumeration of that product space — closed, exhaustive, none repeated (the bijection `_self_test` enforces).
- Geomantic **addition** (Mothers→Daughters→Nieces→Witnesses→Judge), stated by the tradition as *"lines
  different → single (active); lines same → double (passive),"* applied per line, **is bitwise XOR** = addition
  mod 2 on four coordinates. So the 16 under geomantic addition form the **elementary abelian 2-group
  (ℤ/2)⁴**.
  - **Identity = Populus** (∷∷∷∷ = `0000`): `x ⊕ Populus = x`.
  - **Every figure is its own inverse:** `x ⊕ x = Populus` (in ℤ₂, `1⊕1=0`). This is *why* a figure added to
    itself yields Populus.
  - **Closure** is why the Witnesses and Judge are always among the 16; **the Judge always has even parity**
    (an even number of single points) — a known consequence of the group law.

The script's `add_figures` is exactly this XOR; `_self_test` now asserts the group properties. *(Jaulin via
BSOAS/Persée catalogue + Dejean–Ferry appendix; XOR identity independently confirmed: Digital Ambler "On
Geomantic Addition" / en.wikipedia "Geomantic figures".)*

### 5a. A consequence worth keeping — the Witnesses and Judge are the parities of the Mother-matrix

Read the four Mothers as a **4×4 binary matrix** (row *i* = Mother *i*; columns = Fire, Air, Water, Earth).
Because every derivation step is XOR, the whole shield collapses to simple parities (verified empirically
4000/4000 random charts, and provable from the group law):

- **Right Witness = the four column-parities** of the matrix (`RW = M₁⊕M₂⊕M₃⊕M₄`).
- **Left Witness = the four row-parities** (each Left-Witness line *j* = the parity of Mother *j*'s own lines).
- **Judge = column-parity ⊕ row-parity**, line by line.
- **The Judge therefore always has EVEN total parity** — an even number of single points — because
  Σ(column-parities) = Σ(row-parities) = the total parity of the matrix, so they cancel mod 2. This is the
  classical "the Judge must be an even figure" rule, here *derived* rather than asserted.

`ShieldChart.validate()` now enforces all four facts on every chart (it was previously a stub that returned
`True`); a failure means the transpose/addition derivation is broken. *The eight even figures — Populus, Via,
Carcer, Conjunctio, Acquisitio, Amissio, Fortuna Major, Fortuna Minor — are exactly the possible Judges; the
eight odd figures can never be Judge.*

## 6. The element tangle — three distinct schemes (do not conflate)

Element is the messiest attribution in every tradition. There are **three** different things called a figure's
"element," and they disagree on ~8 of 16 figures:

1. **Active-lines** — which of the four element-lines are single/active. This is what `FIGURES.md`'s "Inner
   Element" now lists (factually true to the dot-pattern; a *set*, e.g. Puer = Fire, Air, Earth).
2. **Structural / "inner" ruling element** — a *single* element per figure by a traditional rule (e.g. Fortuna
   Major = Earth, Acquisitio = Air, Albus = Water). Agrippa-ish.
3. **Astrological / "outer" element** — from the figure's zodiac sign (e.g. Fortuna Major = Fire via Leo).
   French web sources mostly use this one.

| Figure | (1) active-lines | (2) structural single | (3) astrological |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fortuna Major | Water, Earth | Earth | Fire |
| Acquisitio | Air, Earth | Air | Fire |
| Amissio | Fire, Water | Fire | Earth |
| Laetitia | Fire | Fire | Water |
| Tristitia | Earth | Earth | Air |
| Rubeus | Air | Air | Water |
| Albus | Water | Water | Air |
| Puella | Fire, Water, Earth | Water | Air |

A **documented split, not an error in any source.** The `FIGURE_DATA["element"]` field in the script is one
defensible choice; cite the scheme when it matters. *(Digital Ambler, "On Elemental Assignments".)*
