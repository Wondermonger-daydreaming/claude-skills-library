# Horary and Na Jia on the same question

In William Lilly's horary, “Will I be promoted?” assigns the office to the planet ruling the tenth-house cusp. In 文王卦 (*Wén Wáng Guà*, King Wen hexagram divination), the same question selects 官鬼 (*guānguǐ*, Officials/Ghosts) as 用神 (*yòngshén*, the focus of this inquiry). In one surviving cast the official line is also 世 (*shì*, self). The applicant and the office occupy **one line**. Asking when two significators will meet would miss the Chinese author's actual argument. [Lilly, pp. 444–445][lilly-office] · [《增刪卜易》, ch. 16][month]

Use this reference when both methods have been requested. Record the same question, the event that would count as its fulfillment, and any deadline. Cast and judge each figure on its own terms before comparing the answers. Keep the raw inputs, the calculated relationships, and the reader's judgment distinguishable. Agreement can describe the two readings. An observed outcome requires separate evidence.

|---|---|---|---|

This skill uses tropical longitudes, Regiomontanus cusps and seven traditional planetary rulers. | Six line values, ordered from the bottom. Record the casting method and the calendar at the cast. Numbers 6 and 9 change, 7 and 8 hold. | **Different operations.** A timestamp and coordinates suffice to erect the horary figure. They leave the six coin or yarrow results undetermined. |
| Who asks? | The ruler of the rising sign (Lord 1), with the Moon as co-significator. The Moon also gives a sequence of separations and applications. | The Eight Palaces (八宮 *bāgōng*) place the self line 世爻 (*shìyáo*) according to the hexagram's position within its palace. ### Three kinds of relationship to keep distinct

**Classification.** In Na Jia, the inner and outer trigrams assign the 天干 (*tiāngān*, Heavenly Stems) and 地支 (*dìzhī*, Earthly Branches) to the lines. The branch assigns the line to one of the 五行 (*wǔxíng*, Five Phases). The palace phase supplies the reference for the following five named classes traditionally called the Six Relatives. This reference phase can differ from the phase of the line marked 世. [Na Jia §§2–5](../../yijing/references/NA_JIA.md) · [《增刪卜易》, ch. 8][use]

| Relation to the palace phase | Relative | Example of a question it can represent |
|---|---|---|
| Line generates palace | 父母 *fùmǔ*, Parents | A parent, a document or a deed |
| Line shares the palace phase | 兄弟 *xiōngdì*, Siblings | A sibling, a peer or a competitor |
| Palace generates line | 子孫 *zǐsūn*, Offspring | A child or pupil |
| Palace controls line | 妻財 *qīcái*, Wife/Wealth | Money or goods |
| Line controls palace | 官鬼 *guānguǐ*, Officials/Ghosts | An office, duty or source of constraint |

The traditional kin names can represent people, objects and activities. Asking about the **office** and asking about the **letter of appointment** can therefore select different relatives. If the selected class appears twice (兩現 *liǎngxiàn*), record both candidates and the reasons for choosing one. If absent from the visible figure, the engine can expose a 伏神 (*fúshén*, hidden spirit) from the pure palace hexagram at the same position. Its availability for use still needs judgment. The implemented rule is in §5.3 of the Na Jia reference, which supersedes its introductory note calling 飛伏 (*fēifú*) deferred. [Na Jia §§5.2–5.6](../../yijing/references/NA_JIA.md)

**Reception and 生克 (*shēngkè*).** In horary the planet whose dignity another occupies is the **receiver**. With the Moon in Aries, the Sun receives the Moon by exaltation. Mutual reception requires a relationship in each direction. Lilly allows mutual reception to accomplish a matter even when the principals have no aspect to one another. In Na Jia, Water generates Wood and Metal controls Wood. Once Wood is selected as 用神, its generator is 元神 (*yuánshén*, supporting spirit) and its controller is 忌神 (*jìshén*, adverse spirit). Direction matters in both traditions, but the operands differ. A planet receives by virtue of a dignity at a zodiacal position. A phase generates or controls another phase wherever their branches occur. A line classified by control can be precisely the office being sought. [Lilly, p. 112][lilly-reception] · [《增刪卜易》, chs. 8–9][spirits]

**Combining and clashing.** 合 (*hé*, combine or bind) and 沖 (*chōng*, clash) operate on named branches. For example, 子–丑 (*zǐ–chǒu*) form a 六合 (*liùhé*, six-pair combination) pair. 子–午 (*zǐ–wǔ*) clash. The six clash pairs are six steps apart in the twelve-branch cycle. A horary opposition is an actual 180° longitude difference with a question of orb and motion. A pair of branch labels supplies neither. Also distinguish one pair from the whole-hexagram designations 六合卦 and 六沖卦 (*liùhéguà*, *liùchōngguà*). The engine tests all three line pairs (1,4), (2,5), (3,6) for the whole-hexagram designation. Chapter 19 distinguishes a combination that raises a static line from one that ties up a moving line. Chapter 17 distinguishes a day clash that activates a strong static line (暗動 *àndòng*) from one that breaks a weak one. A lookup such as “合 = trine = yes” loses both distinctions. [《增刪卜易》, ch. 17][day] · [ch. 19][combine] · [ch. 20][clash]

### How far can an analogy go?

A **house** divides local celestial space. An 八宮 palace groups eight hexagrams under a pure hexagram and fixes their reference phase. The twelve Earthly Branches can participate in calendrical and directional schemes. Sharing the number twelve with the zodiac does not make them longitude sectors in this operation.

A translator of light separates from one principal and applies to the other. A collector receives their aspects, and Lilly also specifies that both principals receive the heavier planet in dignity. 三合 brings together three named branches without a sequence of planetary contacts. Likewise, a void-of-course Moon concerns forthcoming application, whereas 旬空 follows from the branches missing in a ten-day series. Both can prompt a timing question, but each requires its own inputs. [Lilly, p. 112][lilly-reception] · [p. 126][lilly-collection] · [Chinese void table][void]

### What counts as time?

- **Horary.** Establish whether a route to perfection exists. The ephemeris can date an exact aspect. In the skill's timing convention the remaining degrees can also signify a number of units, whose scale depends on sign modality, angular/succedent/cadent house, speed and the timescale of the matter. “Exact in eight days” and “appointed in eight days” are different claims. [Horary timing](TIMING.md) · [Lilly, p. 125][lilly-perfect]
- **Wen Wang Gua.** Month and day help judge a line *now* and identify a possible 應期 (*yìngqī*, time of fulfillment). In the branch-month convention, solar-term boundaries set the month, e.g. 寅 (*yín*) begins at 立春 (*lìchūn*). The day belongs to a sixty-day 干支 (*gānzhī*, stem–branch) cycle. Each ten-day 旬 (*xún*) leaves two branches unrepresented, giving 旬空 (*xúnkōng*, void for that ten-day period). A later branch may fill a void, release a binding combination or meet a clash. Direction and duration depend on the question and the rule invoked. Record the calendrical convention, especially near boundaries. With only six line values one can compute the chart and leave the timing open. [《卜筮正宗》, vol. 1][solar] · [《增刪卜易》, ch. 16][month] · [ch. 26][void]

## Worked question · Will I obtain promotion to this office?

The Chinese manual records an official asking about advancement. For horary, reuse the repository's **existing Greenwich test instant** and change the quesited to house 10. These are two figures for the same question. Only the Chinese source reports an outcome.

### Horary · follow the planets past the snapshot

At 12:00 UTC on 1 January 2024, Greenwich (51.4779° N, 0.0015° W), Aries 24°40′ rises. Lord 1 is **Mars**, at 27°41′ Sagittarius in house 9. The **Moon**, at 11°54′ Virgo in house 6, co-signifies the applicant. The tenth cusp is 9°47′ Capricorn, making **Saturn**, at 3°17′ Pisces in house 12, the office. Mars has term dignity but is under the Sun's beams. Saturn has face dignity and is cadent. Lilly's chapter on office explicitly assigns the first and tenth and considers reception, third-party intervention and impediment. [Lilly, pp. 444–445][lilly-office]

The engine flags an applying Mars–Saturn sextile, 5.61° from exact, and possible prior contacts with Venus and Jupiter. It labels the Moon void of course. A forward check with the same Swiss Ephemeris gives the following sequence (UTC, nearest minute).

| Time in January 2024 | Event | Consequence for the reading |
|---|---|---|
| 1st, 13:26 | Venus squares Saturn | A prior contact with the office is real. Its role as prohibition still calls for judgment. |
| 2nd, 08:54 and 22:13 | Moon squares Mercury, then Mars | Both occur before the Moon enters Libra on the 3rd at 00:47. Under the skill's before-sign-exit rule, the void flag fails. |
| 4th, 14:58 | Mars enters Capricorn | At casting, Sagittarius and Pisces stand in square by sign. The sextile perfects from Capricorn, where Mars occupies Saturn's domicile. |
| 10th, 02:40 | Mars sextiles Saturn exactly | The later contact is real. Jupiter has **no exact sextile with Saturn** between casting and this event despite its smaller initial orb. |

**Reading.** A direct path to the office appears after Mars changes condition. Saturn can then receive Mars by domicile. Venus reaches Saturn first, so there is an obstacle or intervening interest to weigh. This is a conditional prospect of advancement, with no warrant for announcing an appointment on 10 January. The engine ranks some candidates by remaining degrees, uses current speeds to estimate time, and searches within the present moieties for the Moon's next application. Those shortcuts are consequential in this figure. [Judgment engine](../scripts/judge.py) · [Horary aspects](ASPECTS.md)

### Wen Wang Gua · the office already occupies self

The recorded cast falls in 寅月 (*yín yuè*, Yín month), on 丙申日 (*bǐngshēn rì*). 艮 (*Gèn*, no. 52, Mountain over Mountain) changes to 頤 (*Yí*, no. 27). Reconstructing the line marks gives **6, 8, 9, 8, 8, 7** from the bottom. The palace is 艮, Earth. Wood controls Earth, so its Wood line carries 官鬼. The original and transformed line data below are the engine's reconstruction. The historical diagram itself omits the line stems. [Chinese case text][month] · [1925 facsimile, vol. 1, printed p. 43, PDF image 63][scan]

| Line | Original stem–branch | Relative | Role and change |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 丙辰 *bǐngchén*, Earth | 兄弟 | Moving → 庚子 *gēngzǐ*, Water, 妻財. The original Earth controls its transform. |
| 2 | 丙午 *bǐngwǔ*, Fire | 父母 | Static |
| 3 | 丙申 *bǐngshēn*, Metal | 子孫 | 應, moving → 庚辰 *gēngchén*, Earth, 兄弟. Earth feeds the original Metal (回頭生 *huítóushēng*). |
| 4 | 丙戌 *bǐngxū*, Earth | 兄弟 | Static |
| 5 | 丙子 *bǐngzǐ*, Water | 妻財 | Static |
| 6 | 丙寅 *bǐngyín*, Wood | 官鬼 | 世 and 用神 together. Static, supported by its own month branch. |

The author's difficulty is specific. 申 (*shēn*) day clashes with 寅 on line 6, and Metal controls its Wood. Yet the author reads 申–子–辰 (*shēn–zǐ–chén*) as a 三合 (*sānhé*, three-branch combination) forming Water, which feeds the office's Wood. The original figure is a six-clash hexagram. Neither that label nor the hostile day settles the judgment. The author takes the office as attainable and explains the third month by 辰 Earth leaving emptiness. 丙申 belongs to the ten-day series beginning 甲午 (*jiǎwǔ*), whose void branches are 辰 and 巳. The day, month and three-branch judgment are supplied by the text and checked separately from the engine's line assignments. [Case and diagram][month] · [Void table][void]

**Two readings in the witnesses.** On printed p. 43 the 1925 edition puts **兄酉金** (*xiōng, yǒu jīn*, Sibling, Yǒu Metal) beside the changing third line. The online chapter gives **兄弟辰土** (*xiōngdì, chén tǔ*, Sibling, Chén Earth), which agrees with the lower 震 (*Zhèn*) trigram of no. 27 and the original Earth palace. The print forecasts **二月** (second month), then reports and explains **三月** (third month). The transcription forecasts and reports the third month. Record the conflict. The algorithm provides a reason to prefer 辰 for the line calculation. It cannot establish the original wording, or independently confirm the reported promotion in Yunnan. [1925 scan, image 63][scan] · [Online transcription, ch. 16][month]

## Reproduce the mechanical parts

Run from the repository root with Python 3. The horary caster requires `pyswisseph`. The fixed cast below uses the Yijing engine's normal line objects and bypasses random casting.

```sh
python3 skills/horary/scripts/judge.py --datetime '2024-01-01 12:00:00' --tz 0 \
  --lat 51.4779 --lon -0.0015 --location Greenwich --topic career \
  --question 'Will I obtain promotion to this office?'

PYTHONPATH=skills/yijing/scripts python3 - <<'PY'
from cast_hexagram import Hexagram, Line
from na_jia import build_chart, format_chart

values = [6, 8, 9, 8, 8, 7]
cast = Hexagram([Line(value, pos) for pos, value in enumerate(values, 1)])
print(cast.number, cast.get_relating_hexagram().number)
print(format_chart(build_chart(cast, topic="career")))
PY
```

**Implementation boundaries.** `na_jia.py` assigns positions, suggests a topic relative, detects paired branches and derives moving-line feedback. Its topic keyword cannot choose between competing claims in the text. The cited engine does not adjudicate calendar strength, the three-branch Water configuration or an outcome. In horary, also check **exaltation reception by hand** at this revision. With Sun in Gemini and Moon in Aries, `judge.reception` prints “Moon receives Sun by exaltation”. The Sun owns exaltation in Aries, so the **Sun receives the Moon**. The function's domicile direction is correct. A passing built-in test does not exercise this example. [Na Jia engine](../../yijing/scripts/na_jia.py) · [Horary judgment engine](../scripts/judge.py) · [Dignity table](DIGNITIES.md)

## What the witnesses establish

| Witness | What it supports, and where the inference stops |
|---|---|
| William Lilly, *Christian Astrology*, London, 1647, “To the Reader”, pp. 111–112, 124–126, 444–445 | Lilly names predecessors including Dariot and Bonatti. The printed pages describe reception, translation, collection and a judgment of office. These are direct witnesses to Lilly's practice. [Preface][preface] · [Wellcome copy][lilly-book] |
| 《漢書》卷七十五 (*Hànshū*, juàn 75), biography of 京房 (*Jīng Fáng*) | Describes the teaching of 焦延壽 (*Jiāo Yánshòu*), the 64 hexagrams taking turns over days, weather portents, and Jing's use of the method. This supports an early calendrical practice. The biography supplies no line table for the promotion case. [Biography][hanshu] |
| Received 《京氏易傳》 (*Jīngshì Yìzhuàn*), 四庫全書 recension, upper juan, 乾 (*Qián*) | The text specifies Metal, 甲 and 壬, and line 3 as 應. Its annotations locate 壬戌 at 世 and label 甲寅 Wood as wealth. Compare `python3 skills/yijing/scripts/na_jia.py 1` (世 6, 應 3, 甲寅 at line 2). The Qing editors credit Jing and the commentator 陸績 (*Lù Jì*), and discuss earlier catalogues with different extents. This adds a direct check of a **received witness** to the repository's earlier access log. Dating each rule or annotation to the historical Jing remains open. [乾 entry][jing] · [Editors' notice][siku] |
| 《增刪卜易》 (*Zēngshān Bǔyì*), chs. 6, 8, 16–20, 26 | A later manual gives procedures for choosing the focus, handling combinations, and judging events. The case preserves a practitioner's interpretation and a reported outcome. The 1925 printing and online text preserve variant readings of one reported event. [Position rule][self] · [Topic rule][use] · [Printed witness][scan] |
| Jeffrey Kotyk, *The Sinicization of Indo-Iranian Astrology in Medieval China* (2018), pp. 1–2 | A secondary study traces specific horoscopic material into China through Indo-Iranian intermediaries. That documented contact concerns horoscopy. It supplies no transmission chain between Lilly's significator rules and Jing's line assignments. [Study][kotyk] |

For these particular mechanisms, independent development in separately attested traditions is a defensible working account. The shared problem of selecting a representative explains the functional analogy. A historical influence claim needs a datable intermediary text or practice transmitting the rule itself. The worked question leaves each reader with a concrete test. **When can Mars reach Saturn, and who gets there first? What can sustain the office line already at 世?**
